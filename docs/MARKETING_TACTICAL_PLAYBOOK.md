## Quick Reference for Execution

## Section 1: Content Creation Checklist

### Blog Post Publishing Process

- [ ] **Topic Selected** (tie to business goal + SEO keyword)
- [ ] **Outline Created** (3-5 main sections)
- [ ] **Draft Written** (2,000-4,000 words, include examples)
- [ ] **Internal Review** (clarity, accuracy, tone)
- [ ] **SEO Optimized** (keyword in title, headers, meta description)
- [ ] **Visual Assets** (1-3 images/diagrams)
- [ ] **CTA Added** (newsletter signup, product signup, or demo)
- [ ] **Scheduled on Medium, Hashnode** (auto-crosspost setup)
- [ ] **LinkedIn Post** (thread or standalone post linking to blog)
- [ ] **Email Announcement** (Substack newsletter)
- [ ] **WhatsApp Communities** (share in relevant communities)
- [ ] **Slack/Discord** (relevant communities)

**Time to Publish**: 5-7 days from topic selection

---

### LinkedIn Posting Checklist

**Before Posting:**
- [ ] **Hook** (First line is compelling question or statement)
- [ ] **Value** (Does it educate, inspire, or help?)
- [ ] **Engagement** (Question at end to prompt replies)
- [ ] **Hashtags** (3-5 relevant hashtags)
- [ ] **Mentions** (Tag 3-5 relevant people)
- [ ] **Formatting** (Line breaks, bullet points for readability)
- [ ] **Timing** (Post 8-9 AM on Tuesday-Thursday)
- [ ] **Image/Video** (Visual content 10x more engagement)

**After Posting:**
- [ ] **Monitor Comments** (Reply within 1 hour to first 5 comments)
- [ ] **Share to Story** (if important content)
- [ ] **Respond to All Comments** (within 24 hours)
- [ ] **Engage on Others' Posts** (Like/comment on related posts)

**Frequency**: 3-4 posts per week for Founder

## Section 2: Event & Conference Guide

### Speaking Opportunity Checklist

**Finding Opportunities:**
- [ ] Identify target conferences (3-month advance)
- [ ] Research CFP (Call for Proposals) deadline
- [ ] Prepare speaker one-sheet (2 topics, bio, photo)
- [ ] Submit 2-3 talk proposals simultaneously
- [ ] Follow up 2 weeks after submission

**After Talk Accepted:**
- [ ] Confirm date, time, format, audience size
- [ ] Request: audience list, marketing mentions, recording rights
- [ ] Create slide deck (tell story, 1 key idea per slide)
- [ ] Practice presentation 5+ times
- [ ] Record video version (YouTube, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Prepare speaking points document

**After Talk:**
- [ ] Collect attendee emails (if allowed)
- [ ] Share recording on YouTube, LinkedIn, blog
- [ ] Write blog post about key insights
- [ ] Quote attendee testimonials in social media
- [ ] Pitch follow-up press release to media

**Target**: 2-3 speaking slots per quarter

---

### Conference Sponsorship ROI

**Sponsorship Tier Decision**:
- **Gold/Platinum**: $5K-15K (booth, speaking slot, branding)
  - Best for: 500+ attendee conferences
  - ROI target: 10 leads minimum
  
- **Silver/Bronze**: $2K-5K (booth, no speaking)
  - Best for: 200-500 attendee conferences
  - ROI target: 5-8 leads

- **Community Partner**: Free-$1K (no booth, mention)
  - Best for: Very targeted, small audiences
  - ROI target: 3-5 leads

**Lead Capture Strategy**:
- Booth: Demo booth with lead capture (tablet form)
- Social: Live-tweet conference with branded hashtag
- Content: Offer free resource for emails
- Follow-up: Immediate email after conference

## Section 3: Newsletter Strategy

### Substack Newsletter Format

**Publish**: Every Friday morning (8-9 AM timezone)

**Structure** (15-20 min read):
1. **Intro** (Personal hook, 2-3 sentences)
2. **Main Insight** (1 big idea, 400-600 words)
   - Why it matters
   - Real example
   - Actionable takeaway
3. **Industry News** (2-3 relevant news items with context)
4. **Learnings** (1 lesson from week, 100-200 words)
5. **CTA** (Sign up for demo / Join community / Read full article)

**Growth Strategy**:
- Cross-promote on LinkedIn
- Add CTA to blog posts
- Share in WhatsApp communities
- Guest swap with other newsletters
- Monthly referral incentive

**Target**: 500 → 2,500 → 5,000 subscribers in 12 months

## Section 4: Community Building (WhatsApp)

### Community Purpose & Setup

**Communities by Type**:

1. **"Power Users"** - Product users, feedback
   - Invite: Customers + early adopters
   - Cadence: Daily engagement
   - Admin: Product or CS lead
   - Posts: Feature updates, tips, wins

2. **"Founders Circle"** - Founder-exclusive content
   - Invite: Other founders (network building)
   - Cadence: Weekly
   - Admin: Founder
   - Posts: Unfiltered thoughts, behind-the-scenes

3. **"Sales Ops Professionals"** - Industry community
   - Invite: Non-customers, industry peers
   - Cadence: 2-3x weekly
   - Admin: Marketing/community lead
   - Posts: Industry news, best practices

4. **"EMEA Community"** - Geographic group (2027)
   - Invite: Customers + prospects in region
   - Cadence: Localized content + timezone-friendly
   - Admin: EMEA team member
   - Posts: Regional news, events, case studies

### Community Engagement Calendar

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Monday | Weekly insights (3-4 min read) | Founder |
| Tuesday | Industry news + discussion | Community lead |
| Wednesday | Customer win/case study | CSM |
| Thursday | Product feature deep dive | Product |
| Friday | Weekend reading link | Marketing |

### Community Growth Targets

- Month 1: 50 members (direct invites)
- Month 3: 150 members (word of mouth)
- Month 6: 300 members (active recruitment)
- Month 12: 500+ members (community of practice)

## Section 5: Press & Media Strategy

### Media Outreach Process

**Step 1: Identify Journalists**
- Use HARO (Help A Reporter Out) for journalist requests
- Follow relevant journalists on Twitter
- Subscribe to industry newsletters

**Step 2: Prepare Story Angle**
- Problem/trend that's newsworthy
- Unique FounderOS angle
- Data/stats to support (if available)
- Customer quote (with permission)

**Step 3: Pitch Email**
- Subject: Newsworthy angle (not "feature FounderOS")
- Personalization: Reference journalist's recent articles
- Keep short: 100-150 words
- Include 2 expert quotes
- Offer: More info, data, interview

**Step 4: Follow Up**
- No response after 3 days = 1 follow-up
- No response after 5 days = Move on
- Track all pitches in spreadsheet

**Step 5: After Coverage**
- Thank journalist on social media
- Share article in all channels
- Request permission to share quote on sales materials
- Add to press section of website

**Target**: 10+ media mentions per year

## Section 6: SEO & Organic Growth

### Blog SEO Checklist

**Before Publishing**:
- [ ] Keyword research (target: 500-2K monthly searches)
- [ ] Title includes keyword (60 characters max)
- [ ] Meta description includes keyword (160 characters)
- [ ] URL is slug of title (no numbers/dates)
- [ ] Internal links to 2-3 related posts
- [ ] External links to 2-3 authority sources
- [ ] Image alt text includes keyword

**After Publishing**:
- [ ] Submit to Google Search Console
- [ ] Share on social media
- [ ] Include in newsletter
- [ ] Add to related blog posts (as "See also")
- [ ] Update homepage if top post

**Keywords to Target** (Examples):
- "Revenue forecasting with AI" (500 searches/month)
- "Autonomous sales workflows" (300 searches/month)
- "CSM health scoring" (400 searches/month)
- "Sales pipeline prediction" (600 searches/month)

- Month 6: 15K monthly visitors

## Section 7: Paid Advertising Strategy

### LinkedIn Advertising

**Campaign Setup**:
- Budget: $2K-5K/month (start)
- Target audience:
  - VP Sales / VP Revenue
  - Sales Ops / Revenue Ops
  - Chief Revenue Officer
  - Companies: 500-5000 employees, B2B SaaS

**Campaign Types**:
1. **Lead Gen Ads** (newsletter/demo signup)
   - CPC: $5-15
   - Target: 100-200 leads/month
   
2. **Video Views** (founder content, product tour)
   - CPM: $3-8
   - Target: 50K-100K views/month
   
3. **Website Traffic** (blog posts, case studies)
   - CPC: $2-8
   - Target: 2K-5K clicks/month

**Best Practices**:
- Test 3-5 ad creative versions
- Keep copy < 150 characters
- Use compelling visuals (people/data)
- Bid strategy: Automated
- Conversion tracking: Enabled
- Review weekly, optimize daily

### Google Ads (Later - Month 6+)

**High-Intent Keywords**:
- "Revenue forecasting software"
- "Sales forecasting AI"
- "Revenue operations platform"
- "Customer success software"

## Section 8: Customer Advocacy & Testimonials

### Testimonial Collection Process

**Timing**: 30-60 days after customer success achieved

**Template Email**:
```
Hi [Customer Name],

You've been amazing to work with, and we'd love to share 
your success with others in the revenue ops community.

Would you be willing to:
1. Give us a 30-second testimonial (video or text)?
2. Share a short case study about your results?
3. Connect me with other teams at your company?

Here's what we achieved together:
- [Metric 1 improvement]
- [Metric 2 improvement]
- [Metric 3 improvement]

Let me know what works best for you!
```

### Case Study Development

**Structure**:
1. **Challenge** (1 paragraph - their problem before FounderOS)
2. **Solution** (1 paragraph - how FounderOS addressed it)
3. **Results** (3-5 metrics with before/after)
4. **Quote** (2-3 sentence customer quote)
5. **CTA** (Schedule demo link)

**Format**: 1-2 page PDF + blog post + LinkedIn post + sales asset

**Target**: 3-4 case studies per quarter
