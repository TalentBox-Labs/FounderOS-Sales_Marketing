## 9. Discovery Call Framework

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for taking time. I know you're busy. Quick goal for today: 
Understand what's keeping you awake at night, revenue-wise. 
Not a pitch — just conversation. Sound good?"

### Understanding Their World (8 min)
Key questions:
1. "Walk me through your revenue process. How do forecasting, pipeline, churn, 
   expansion typically go?"
2. "Where does it break down? Where do you feel most pain?"
3. "What's that costing you in dollars?"
4. "How are you trying to solve it today?"
5. "What would it look like if that were fixed?"

### Relevant Problem Identification (5 min)
"Based on what you said, sounds like [PROBLEM] is your biggest challenge.
Do I have that right?"

If yes: Move to solution brief
If no: "What is?"

### Solution Brief (3 min)
"Here's what we typically do with companies like yours:
1. Connect your data
2. AI learns your patterns
3. Automated workflows + alerts
4. You close more deals, prevent churn, find expansion

Timeline: 30 days to first impact"

### Closing
"Make sense? Worth a deeper dive?"

If yes: "Great. Let's schedule 30 min next week so I can show you 
specifically how this would work with your data."

If no: "No problem. When would be a better time?"
