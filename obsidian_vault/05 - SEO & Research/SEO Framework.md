# 🔍 SEO Framework

## Keyword Strategy
WorkCrew targets **commercial investigation** and **informational** intent keywords in the tech hiring space.

## Default Research Gate (research_mapper validator)
The following terms are checked by default in `03_Research.md`:
- linkedin recruiter
- indeed
- ziprecruiter
- pricing
- pros / cons / best for

The following terms are checked in `02_SEO_Plan.md`:
- alternatives to linkedin recruiter
- linkedin recruiter alternatives
- better job descriptions attract more applicants
- understand which skills to prioritize
- frequently asked questions

## CTA Variants
One of these must appear in the SEO plan:
- try workcrew free
- try workcrew
- explore workcrew
- create your workcrew profile

## Funnel Stages
| Stage | Intent | CTA |
|-------|--------|-----|
| Awareness | Informational | soft CTA |
| Consideration | Commercial investigation | try free |
| Decision | Transactional | sign up |
