# 👤 Persona — Technical Recruiter

## Who They Are
- In-house or agency recruiter filling software engineering roles
- Using LinkedIn Recruiter, Indeed, or a mix of tools
- Frustrated by signal-to-noise ratio in applications
- Budget-conscious, especially post-2024 hiring freezes

## What They Want
- Structured process that reduces chaos
- Less time sourcing, more time evaluating
- Faster time-to-offer without surprises
- Hiring manager alignment on requirements up front

## What They Fear
- Making a bad hire that reflects poorly on them
- Losing a great candidate to a faster offer
- Being blindsided at the offer stage

## Content Implications
- Lead with practical tips, not theory
- Use concrete examples with named platforms (LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter)
- CTAs around "structured process" or "try free" resonate more than "enterprise solution"
