# 🎙️ Brand Voice Guide

## Core Principles
- **Direct** — no fluff, no filler
- **Practitioner-first** — recruiters and HMs are smart; speak to them as peers
- **Outcome-oriented** — every claim must connect to a hiring outcome

## Banned Phrases
Automatically blocked by validators:

| Phrase | Why |
|--------|-----|
| game-changer | vague hype |
| cutting-edge | overused |
| revolutionary | unsubstantiated |
| in today's fast-paced world | filler opener |
| best platform in the world | unverifiable |
| guaranteed hiring | misleading |
| 100% success rate | false claim |
